SentinelAI – AI Financial Crime Investigation Platform
One-line Pitch

Built an AI-powered financial crime investigation platform that autonomously detects suspicious transaction patterns, reconstructs fraud cases, explains AI decisions, and guides analysts through an interactive investigation experience instead of traditional dashboards.

Product Vision

Traditional fraud systems only answer:

Is this transaction fraudulent?

SentinelAI answers:

What happened?
Why did it happen?
Who is involved?
How are entities connected?
What evidence supports the conclusion?
What should the analyst do next?

Instead of giving analysts another dashboard, SentinelAI creates an interactive investigation room where every fraud incident becomes a guided case.

SentinelAI is an AI-native investigation platform.

It combines:

Data Engineering: Kafka event streams, Neo4j graph modeling
Machine Learning: Fraud detection and explainability
Agentic AI: Multi-agent investigation workflows with LangGraph
Software Engineering: Full-stack architecture using React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, and Docker
Product Design: A Netflix-inspired case investigation experience, where analysts navigate investigations as sequential "episodes" instead of interacting with a conventional dashboard.

That final point is the signature "wow factor": the UI isn't just visually different—it changes how the user experiences fraud analysis, making the project memorable while remaining technically grounded and implementable.
